📖 City Builder — Help

The Basics

  • Roads first. Every building needs a road tile touching it.
  • Construction takes years. Watch the progress bar.
  • Landmarks arrive when history says.

Government

  • Government type affects income, growth, military, and happiness.
  • Switch government any time from the Gov tab — costs money and approval.
  • Drafting converts civilians to soldiers during war — big military boost, big happiness hit.
  • Approval below 25% risks unrest. Below 15% can lose elections.

Military & War

  • Each city starts with different base defense. Wealthy cities like Singapore and New York have strong starting armies.
  • You can declare war on rival world powers from the Army tab.
  • Winning a war gives reparations (money), approval boost, and a Victory Monument.
  • Losing a war costs money, damages buildings, and drops approval heavily.
  • Historical wars (WWI, WWII) fire automatically.

Controls

  • Space pause · Esc cancel
  • Pinch or wheel to zoom. Two-finger drag to pan.
