📖 City Builder — Help

The Basics

  • Roads first. Every building needs a road tile touching it. Red ! dots mean no access — nothing works.
  • Balance jobs & homes. People only move in when both exist and are reachable.
  • Time passes by the day. Watch the calendar. Speed up (up to 16×) to see history unfold.
  • Construction takes time. Big buildings like airports take years to finish.
  • Landmarks are free in Realistic mode and cannot be demolished.
  • Little people walk along your roads. Cars appear as population and traffic grow.

HUD Stats Explained

Date
Current year, month and day. Each game tick advances time based on speed.
Population
Total residents. Grows when housing + jobs + wellbeing are balanced.
Jobs
Workplaces from all buildings. Mismatch causes unemployment or labour shortage.
Homes
Housing capacity. Overcrowding lowers wellbeing.
Wellbeing
Happiness 0–100. Driven by education, healthcare, transit, culture, safety, environment and jobs.
Environment
Air and water quality 0–100. Damaged by pollution; improved by parks, forests, wetlands, recycling.
Traffic
Congestion. Grows with population; reduced by transit and arterial roads.
Energy / Water
Supply vs demand. Shortages cause blackouts and rationing.

Service Coverage

Six services show in the Report. Each is % of population covered; higher coverage raises wellbeing:

  • 🎓 Education — schools, libraries, universities
  • ❤️ Healthcare — clinics, hospitals
  • 🚇 Transit — weighted by population near the stop
  • 🎭 Culture — museums, theatres, music venues
  • 🛡️ Safety — fire and police stations
  • 🏛️ Civic — city hall, plaza, community centres

Landmarks

In Realistic Mode each city starts with a real-world landmark pre-placed on the map. Landmarks cannot be demolished, cost nothing, and give big permanent bonuses.

Controls Cheat Sheet

  • Space pause / unpause
  • Esc cancel current build
  • ↑↓←→ pan when zoomed in
  • Pinch with two fingers to zoom in/out
  • Two-finger drag to pan
  • Drag while a road is selected to lay a line
