📖 City Builder — Help

The Basics

  • Roads first. Every building needs a road tile touching it. Red ! dots mean no access.
  • Construction takes years. Big buildings like airports and nuclear plants take the longest. Watch the progress bar on the map.
  • Time passes by the day. Watch the calendar. Speed up (up to 16×) to see history unfold.
  • Landmarks arrive when history says. Singapore's Merlion, for example, appears in 1972 — not before.
  • Little people and cars walk along your roads. Their numbers scale with population and traffic.

HUD Stats Explained

Date
Current year, month, day. Each tick advances based on speed.
Population
Total residents. Grows when housing, jobs and wellbeing are balanced.
Jobs
Workplaces. Mismatch causes unemployment or labour shortage.
Homes
Housing capacity. Overcrowding lowers wellbeing.
Wellbeing
Happiness 0–100. Driven by education, healthcare, transit, culture, safety, environment and jobs.
Environment
Air and water quality 0–100. Damaged by pollution; improved by parks, forests, wetlands, recycling.
Traffic
Congestion. Reduced by transit and arterial roads.
Energy / Water
Supply vs demand. Shortages cause blackouts and rationing.

Service Coverage

Six services show in the Report. Higher coverage raises wellbeing:

  • 🎓 Education — schools, libraries, universities
  • ❤️ Healthcare — clinics, hospitals
  • 🚇 Transit — weighted by population near the stop
  • 🎭 Culture — museums, theatres, music venues
  • 🛡️ Safety — fire and police stations
  • 🏛️ Civic — city hall, plaza, community centres

Landmarks

In Realistic Mode, each city gets its real-world landmark when it was built historically. Landmarks cannot be demolished, cost nothing, and give big permanent bonuses.
